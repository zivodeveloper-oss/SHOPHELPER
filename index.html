100% FREE UTILITY • 18+

Welcome to Zivo ShopHelper, a free retail utility designed to help local shops manage point-of-sale billing, stock inventory, customer khata, returns, and supplier orders. By continuing, I know that I am 18 plus (18 years of age or older) and legally eligible to use this application and create or manage a shop profile.

This tool is provided free of cost as a shop helper without paid plan lock-ins. Please double-check item prices, quantities, GST percentages, discounts, customer credit balances, and payment modes manually before saving or sharing any bill, and be cautious of third-party scams, phishing links, or fake payment requests.

By default, your shop records are stored locally in your browser on your current device, so please export a JSON backup regularly to protect against browser data resets. If you connect Firebase Cloud Sync, your shop records can also sync to Cloud Firestore under your chosen sync key. For a personalized experience, your username, email, and shop name are used in the app, and the creator of Zivo ShopHelper can view account and cloud details when cloud sync or account features are used. For support or bug reports, contact zivo.developer@gmail.com.
