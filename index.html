100% FREE UTILITY

1. Free Utility & Age Eligibility (18+ Only)

Zivo ShopHelper is a free retail helper for local shops to manage POS billing, stock inventory, customer khata, returns, and supplier orders. You must be at least 18 years old to accept these terms and create or use a shop profile.

2. Support Contact

If you experience any bug, issue, or question while using this app, please email us at zivo.developer@gmail.com so we can help fix it directly. Nothing in these terms limits anyone's lawful rights.

3. Third-Party Fraud & Manual Calculation Check

We are not responsible for scams, phishing links, or fake payment requests from third parties. Please always verify item prices, quantities, GST, discounts, and customer credit balances before finalizing any bill.

4. Local Storage, Firebase Cloud Sync & Profile Details

By default, your shop data is saved locally in your browser on your device—please export a JSON backup regularly so your data is safe if browser storage is cleared. If you connect your own Firebase Cloud config in Firebase Sync, your shop records can sync to your Firebase Firestore project. For a personalized experience, we ask for your username, email, and shop name; note that the creator of Zivo ShopHelper can view account and cloud details when cloud sync or account features are used.
